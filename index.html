How I work

The best people teams don't exist to create more process. They exist to remove unnecessary process and make the right things easier to do.

People first. Start with the human experience, then design the system around it.
Clarity creates confidence. People do their best work when expectations are simple, visible, and easy to follow.
Kindness drives performance. High-performing teams grow when people feel challenged, supported, and genuinely cared for.
Build with people, not for them. The best solutions are created alongside the people who will actually use them.
Make complexity disappear. Great systems feel simple because someone did the hard work behind the scenes.
Leave it better than you found it. Every process, team, leader, and employee experience should be stronger because you were there.

Great work doesn't happen by accident. Clarity helps people perform. Kindness helps them grow. Thoughtful systems help great people do both, and give them a reason to stay.
